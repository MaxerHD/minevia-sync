Minevia 0.4.7 adds a fifth mode, «Freestyle Battle», on Forge 1.20.1.

The mode has its own instance `forge-1.20.1-freestyle` and list `manifest-freestyle.json`: Embeddium, Oculus (shaders),
FerriteCore, ModernFix, Entity Culling, Dynamic FPS, Jade, Simple Voice Chat and ten resource packs (Faithful 64x, Reimagined,
Fresh Textures, Better Leaves, Undopia 3D Doors, Borderless Glass, Low on Fire, Simple Hotbar, BetterHotbar+, Spawned Eggs).
The Freestyle Battle mod itself (by VKM, all rights reserved, https://t.me/vkmmods_bot) is not in this repository and is not
downloaded by the launcher: each player puts `FreestyleBattle-*.jar` into the mode's mods folder by hand.

Choosing a mode now switches the game version and loader by itself (FunTime: Fabric 1.21.11, Freestyle Battle: Forge 1.20.1,
the others: Fabric 1.21.1). The skins tab fits the figures to the cards. New characters: MakserHD, Bekas, Bekzhan, Farhat.

Existing users run Minevia-Setup.exe once to get launcher 0.4.7.
